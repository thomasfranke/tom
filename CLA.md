## Plain-language summary

This summary is not the agreement — sections 1–8 are. It is here because you should not have to parse legalese to know what you are agreeing to.

- **You keep the copyright to your contribution.** Nothing is assigned or transferred. You may reuse your own work anywhere, under any terms, forever.
- **You grant the project a broad license to your contribution**, including the right to sublicense it. In practice this means the project may distribute your contribution under different terms in the future, including terms that are not MIT.
- **This cannot be applied retroactively.** Any release already published under MIT remains MIT permanently. If the project's license changes, it changes only for releases after that point — everything before stays free software that anyone may fork and continue.
- **You are confirming the work is yours to give**, and that you are not knowingly contributing someone else's code or patents.

We would rather state this plainly here than leave it to be discovered in a legal file later. If you would rather not grant that right, that is completely understandable — and there is still plenty of room to help: issues, bug reports, design discussion and documentation feedback are all genuinely valuable, and none of them need a CLA.
