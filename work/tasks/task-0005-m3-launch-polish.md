# Polish the MVP for a public launch

**References:** [tasks/roadmap.md#phase-1--mvp](../../docs/tasks/roadmap.md#phase-1--mvp)

What turns a working MVP into something a stranger can try in five
minutes: wikilinks across the space, formatting shortcuts in source mode,
export to PDF and HTML, cloning a repository by URL from the home screen,
installers for the three desktop platforms, and a landing page carrying a
recording of the rendered diff.

Two of these are not products — packaging and marketing — and they are
tracked here because a launch is not done without them. Two others carry
an unresolved choice of their own: no export package and no packaging
tooling has been picked yet.
