# The brand block

The one place in the app where the identity is the content.

**Status:** Planned · Milestone M0

## Rules

- The wordmark is drawn, not the name set in type, and it is placed by its **cap height** ([wordmark](../../../design/brand/wordmark.md)).
- **Behind it runs a commit trunk** — the graph as the product's own mark rather than as decoration, which is what says what this app is before a word is read.
- The trunk is ornament in the sense that nothing depends on it, and the identity in the sense that no other screen has to explain the product.
- It appears on Home and nowhere else. Inside a space the icon is already on the dock, and the breadcrumb names the space instead.
