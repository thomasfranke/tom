# One shape, twice

The whole identity is **a commit on the trunk**: a ring on a vertical line, one
point in the history of a document.

It appears exactly twice, at the same proportions — the trunk is 0.6 of the
ring's stroke in both — so the two marks read as one thing:

- **In the icon**, inside a document. A solid page with its corner folded, the trunk running from the page's top edge to its bottom edge, the commit a little below centre. Documentation *under version control*, which is the whole product.
- **In the wordmark**, as the O of TOM, drawn at the O's ink box with a stroke the width of the letters' stem. The trunk pokes past the cap line and the baseline — the one place the wordmark leaves the text line, on purpose.

Neither mark carries anything else. There is no branch, no fork, no arrow: one
commit is enough to say "history", and it is the only thing that survives at 16
pixels.
